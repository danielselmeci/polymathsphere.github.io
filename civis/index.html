What makes it different

Real people only

One personal account per human being, under your real name. You join by taking the Citizen Oath.

The Dot, not the Like

A Dot means "I was here." Only the author sees how many Dots a post received. There are no public counts and no follower numbers.

You choose the view

Everything, Following or Now Popular. Nothing is ranked for you personally, and the way Now Popular is ordered is published in the app.

What's happening nearby

Posts carry the city they were written in. Browse your city, a distance around you, your country or the world.

Unfiltered

Text and photos as they are. No beauty filters, no AI-generated posts, and one hashtag per post.

Disagreement is welcome

Posts are not removed for being unpopular. Threats, impersonation, scams and illegal content are.

Your identity is protected

Public name. Private details.

Your name and username are public. Your date of birth, place of birth, email and Citizen Number are visible only to you. Civis shows your city on posts, never your exact position, and has no ads and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
