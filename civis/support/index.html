Support

How can we help?

Email support@polymathsphere.com and a person will reply. For account questions, please write from the email address on your account.

Common questions

Why does Civis ask for my real name and date of birth?

Civis is built on one personal account per real person. Your name is public; your date and place of birth, email and Citizen Number are visible only to you.

What is my Citizen Number?

A random, permanent number that identifies your membership, like CIV-7K4M-29QX. It never appears on your posts or public profile. You can see it in Profile → Citizen ID.

Can I change my name or username?

Not in the app. If your name changed or there is a mistake, email us and we will correct it.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot your password?". If you signed up with Apple, use "Sign in with Apple" instead.

What is the Dot?

A Dot means "I was here." It isn't a like or an endorsement. Only the author of a post sees how many Dots it received and who added them.

Why does my post show my city? Can I hide it?

Posts carry the city they were written in so people can see what's happening nearby. Civis never shows your exact position. To post without a city, tap the pin icon in the composer before publishing, or turn off location for Civis in iOS Settings.

How is Now Popular ordered?

The same way for everyone: public posts from the last 72 hours in the area you chose, ranked by Dots divided by how long ago they were posted. The full method is in the app under "How Now Popular is ordered".

How do I report something or block someone?

Tap the ••• on any post, reply or profile and choose Report or Block. Reports are reviewed by people within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, private details, posts, replies, Dots and follows. If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
