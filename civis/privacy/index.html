Privacy Policy

Public name. Private details.

Effective November 14, 2026

The short version

Civis is a social app where people take part under their real name. To make that work, Civis asks for more identity information than most apps, and keeps most of it private. Your name, username, profile and posts are public. Your date of birth, place of birth, email address and Citizen Number are visible only to you. Civis has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and does not sell personal information.

Who we are

Civis is published by Polymath Sphere LLC, a limited liability company registered in Arkansas, United States. Polymath Sphere LLC is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: support@polymathsphere.com.

Location

If you allow location access, Civis uses it for two things: to show posts from your area, and to add your city to posts you publish. Your position is rounded to a grid of roughly 5 km on your device before it is saved with a post, and the city name is looked up through Apple's geocoding service. Your exact position is never stored by Civis or shown to anyone. You can remove the city from any post before publishing, and you can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Civis receives an identifier for your Apple account, the email address you choose to share (which may be Apple's private relay address) and, the first time only, your name, which is used to pre-fill the sign-up form. You still provide your real name and birth details to create your Civis identity.

Identity verification

Civis may, in the future, ask some people to verify their identity, for example with an identity document. Civis does not do this today. Before any such check is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain exactly what is collected, who processes it and how long it is kept.

What Civis does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking of what you do in the app, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information for marketing.
  • No access to your contacts, microphone or photo library. You pick each photo yourself.

Service providers

Civis runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), provided by Google LLC. Your account, profile, private identity details, posts and photos are stored on Google's servers, which may be located in the United States and other countries, under Google's data processing terms. Apple Inc. provides Sign in with Apple and the geocoding service that turns a position into a city name. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; we don't use cookies or analytics here.

When information is disclosed

Civis discloses personal information only when required by law, such as a valid court order, or when necessary to protect someone from serious harm. Content involving the sexual exploitation of minors is reported to the authorities as the law requires.

How long information is kept

Your information is kept for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, private identity details, username, posts, photos, replies, Dots, follows and block list are deleted. Two things remain: a record that your Citizen Number was issued, so the number is never given to anyone else, which is no longer linked to any personal information; and reports, which may be kept as long as needed for safety and legal reasons.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live (including the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, California and other US states, Canada, Brazil and elsewhere), you can:

  • See your information: Profile → Settings shows the private details Civis holds about you.
  • Change your bio and photo: Profile → Edit profile.
  • Correct your name, username or birth details: these can't be changed in the app, because they anchor one account per person. Email support@polymathsphere.com and we will correct them.
  • Delete your account and data: Profile → Settings → Delete account. If you can't access the app, email support@polymathsphere.com from the address on your account.
  • Get a copy of your data, or object to how it is used: email support@polymathsphere.com.

We answer requests within 30 days. EU/UK residents may also contact their local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

Civis processes your account, identity details and content to provide the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Reports, safety measures and the one-account-per-person rule rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and authentic (Art. 6(1)(f)). Location relies on your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings (Art. 6(1)(a)).

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. Private identity details are stored separately from public profiles and can be read only by the account they belong to. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

Age

Civis is for people aged 14 and over. Where local law sets a higher age for agreeing to the use of personal information without a parent's consent, you must meet that age. We do not knowingly allow younger children to create accounts, and we delete such accounts when we learn of them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
