Support

How can we help?

Email support@polymathsphere.com. A real person (the developer) reads every message, usually within two business days. In the app you can also go to Settings → Meet the developer → Contact support, which adds your app version for you.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account ("Continue without an account"), your trips are saved only on that device. Sign in with Apple to keep them in your own iCloud; anything you logged as a guest moves into your account. If you sign out, your trips leave the app but stay safe in your account and iCloud, and they come back when you sign in again.

How do I log a day?

Open your trip and tap "Add today's trio": pick a photo, add a meal or drink, write one sentence, save. You can also tap the nightly reminder, a widget, or say "Log my day in TripLog Mini" to Siri.

Can I log a day I missed?

Yes. In the editor, tap the date and choose any past day. Future dates and trips that haven't started yet can't be logged; the trip opens on Day 1.

How do I share a day to my Instagram Story?

Open a day, choose a card style (Story, Polaroid or Noir) and tap Instagram. The card opens in Instagram's Story editor. Or tap Share to save it to Photos or send it anywhere.

How do widgets and the Live Activity work?

Touch and hold your Home or Lock Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget and search for TripLog Mini. The Live Activity appears by itself during an active trip; turn it off in Settings → Trip on Lock Screen.

Do my trips sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?

Yes, through your own iCloud, when you're signed in with Apple and "Sync with iCloud" is on in Settings. Settings shows when it last synced; if something from another device is missing, tap Reload from iCloud. Your journal never goes through our server.

How do I export my trip?

On a trip, tap the share button for a PDF, Markdown or a recap card. Settings → Export all my data gives you everything as JSON plus your original photos.

What are the postcard terms?

Prices, payment, cancellations, refunds and delivery are explained in the Terms.

How do I delete my data or account?

Settings → Delete account & all data removes every trip and photo from your device and your iCloud. To disconnect Sign in with Apple as well: iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → TripLog Mini → Stop Using.

How do I send a real printed postcard?

Open a day and tap "Send as a Real Postcard", or go to Settings → Send a Postcard to use any photo. Pick a design, write your message, add the address and pay with Apple Pay. The card is printed and mailed for you, postage included, anywhere in the world.

Can I cancel a postcard?

Yes, within 1 hour of ordering, or until the card goes to print just after midnight Eastern Time if that comes first. The app warns you before you order if there's less than an hour left. Open Settings → Sent Postcards, tap the order, and tap Cancel Postcard. Paid orders are refunded minus a $0.50 processing fee, which covers the card payment fee and isn't refundable. Free (promo code) orders cost nothing to cancel. Cards that can't be printed are refunded in full.

I have a problem with my order

Open Settings → Sent Postcards, tap the order, and tap Get Help with This Order. Pick what's wrong and leave your email; we reply within 2 business days. Please include your order number (shown on the order's page) if you email us instead.

How do I track my postcard?

Settings → Sent Postcards shows every order with its order number and status (ordered, printing, mailed). Apple Pay orders appear in the Wallet app under Orders by themselves; for free orders, tap Add to Apple Wallet. If you gave an email, we also email you when it's ordered, when it's mailed (with USPS tracking for US cards) and if it's canceled.

I reinstalled the app or ordered on another device. Where's my order?

Settings → Sent Postcards → the magnifying glass (Find an Order). Enter the order number from your confirmation email or Wallet and the email you used. No number? Tap Email Me My Order Numbers.

Can I send a postcard to any country?

Almost. Cards go worldwide, but occasionally a country isn't available (for example when the post there is suspended). The app only lists countries we can currently mail to.

How do promo codes work?

Tap "Have a promo code?" before paying. Each code has its own limits, such as an end date or one use per device. If you cancel a card, the code can be used again.

What do tips unlock?

Nothing. Tips are optional and simply support the developer. They're handled by Apple, and no subscription is ever involved.

Which languages are supported?

English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) and Hungarian.
