Privacy Policy

Your journal stays yours.

Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

TripLog Mini has no servers, no accounts database, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your journal lives on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. Polymath Sphere LLC never receives your photos, notes, places or identity.

Who we are

TripLog Mini is published by Polymath Sphere LLC, a limited liability company registered in Arkansas, United States. Contact: support@polymathsphere.com.

What the app stores, and where

Trips, daily photos, meal or drink notes, one-sentence summaries and place names are stored on your device. With iCloud sync on, Apple stores a copy in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. A small copy of your latest day is shared with the app's widgets on your device.

Sign in with Apple

If you use Sign in with Apple, the app receives an anonymous user identifier and, only the first time, the name you choose to share. It stays on your device and is used only to keep you signed in. We don't receive or store your email address.

Photos and location

You choose each photo. If you allow photo access, the app reads only photos taken on the day you are logging, to suggest them. If a photo contains GPS data, its coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to look up the city name; the coordinates themselves are not saved.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, memories and Live Activities are scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved.

Purchases

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. We receive no payment details.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself, for example a card to Instagram or a PDF export through the share sheet.

We do not sell or share personal information

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across apps and websites. Because we collect no personal information, there is nothing to sell.

Your rights

Wherever you live (including the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA and other US states, Canada, Brazil and elsewhere), you can access, export, correct and delete your data yourself at any time:

  • Export: Settings → Export all my data (JSON + original photos)
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account & all data (removes everything from the device and your iCloud)
  • Disconnect Sign in with Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → TripLog Mini → Stop Using

Questions or complaints: support@polymathsphere.com. EU/UK users may also contact their local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

Processing happens on your device to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Optional features such as photo access and notifications rely on your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.

Service providers

Apple Inc. provides iCloud storage, geocoding, Sign in with Apple and In-App Purchase under Apple's own privacy policy. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; we don't use cookies or analytics here.

Children

TripLog Mini is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
