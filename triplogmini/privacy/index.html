Privacy Policy

Your journal stays yours.

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version

TripLog Mini has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and you don't need an account. Without an account, your journal lives only on your device; signed in with Apple, it also syncs through your own private iCloud. Polymath Sphere LLC never receives your journal. The exceptions are things you choose to send us: a printed postcard (only what's needed to print, mail and support that order) and a help message about an order.

Who we are

TripLog Mini is published by Polymath Sphere LLC, a limited liability company registered in Arkansas, United States. Contact: support@polymathsphere.com.

What the app stores, and where

Trips, daily photos, meal or drink notes, one-sentence summaries and place names are stored on your device. Each Apple ID that signs in on a device has its own journal there; using the app without an account keeps a separate journal on that device only. When you're signed in and iCloud sync is on, Apple stores a copy in your private iCloud database, which only you can access. Signing out removes the journal from the app's view but leaves it in your iCloud. A small copy of your latest day is shared with the app's widgets on your device.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

Signing in is optional, and you can use the app without it. If you choose to use Sign in with Apple, the app receives an anonymous user identifier and, only the first time, the name you choose to share. It stays on your device and is used only to keep you signed in and to keep each person's journal separate. We don't receive your Apple ID email from Sign in with Apple.

Photos and location

You choose each photo. If you allow photo access, the app reads only photos taken on the day you are logging, to suggest them. If a photo contains GPS data, its coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to look up the city name; the coordinates themselves are not saved.

Notifications and Live Activities

Reminders, memories and Live Activities are scheduled locally on your device. No push servers are involved.

Purchases

Optional tips are processed entirely by Apple through In-App Purchase. Printed postcards are paid with Apple Pay and processed by Stripe. We never see your card number.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your device unless you share it yourself, for example a card to Instagram, a PDF export through the share sheet, or a printed postcard you send.

Printed postcards

Sending a printed postcard is optional. When you send one:

  • What's sent: the card's front and back images, your message, and the recipient's name and postal address go to our postcard server (hosted by Cloudflare), which passes them to PostGrid to print and mail the card. Your own address is never needed; cards carry our company's return address.
  • Payment: Apple Pay, processed by Stripe. We never receive your card number.
  • Your email: Apple Pay shares your email address (or you can type one for a free order). We use it only to send emails about that order: the confirmation, when it's mailed, and if it's canceled or refunded. It also lets you find the order again in the app with its order number. Emails are sent through Resend. We never use it for marketing.
  • Apple Wallet: if the order is added to Wallet, Wallet checks its status (ordered, printing, mailed) with our server.
  • Promo codes: the app sends a random device code, used only to limit how often a code can be used. It isn't linked to you.
  • Help with an order: if you use "Get Help with This Order", we receive your message, the email you enter and the order it's about, and keep them up to 13 months to answer you.
  • How long we keep it: the order record (status, price, country, your email, and the recipient's first name and city) for up to 13 months for support and refunds; then it's deleted automatically. We don't keep the card images or the full address after the card is ordered. PostGrid and Stripe keep their own records as the law requires.
  • Deleting sooner: email support@polymathsphere.com with the order number shown in the app or in Wallet.

We do not sell or share personal information

We do not sell or rent personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across apps and websites. Postcard details are shared only with the companies that print and mail the card and process its payment.

Your rights

Wherever you live (including the EU/EEA and UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA and other US states, Canada, Brazil and elsewhere), you can access, export, correct and delete your data yourself at any time:

  • Export: Settings → Export all my data (JSON + original photos)
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account & all data (removes everything from the device and your iCloud)
  • Disconnect Sign in with Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → TripLog Mini → Stop Using

Questions or complaints: support@polymathsphere.com. EU/UK users may also contact their local data protection authority.

Legal basis (EU/UK)

Processing happens on your device to provide the service you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Printed postcards are processed to fulfil your order (Art. 6(1)(b)). Optional features such as photo access and notifications rely on your consent, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.

Service providers

Apple Inc. provides iCloud storage, geocoding, Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase, Apple Pay and Wallet under Apple's own privacy policy. For printed postcards only: Cloudflare hosts our postcard server, PostGrid prints and mails the cards, Stripe processes payments, and Resend delivers order emails. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; we don't use cookies or analytics here.

Children

TripLog Mini is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU), and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on this page, with a new effective date.
