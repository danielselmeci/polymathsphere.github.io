GO: Travel App

Travel intelligence for modern travelers

GO: Travel App provides intelligent travel planning and management tools designed for travelers who value precision, efficiency, and control.

TripLog Mini

A travel journal for iPhone, built by the Polymath Sphere studio

TripLog Mini turns each day of a trip into three things: one photo, one meal or drink, and one sentence. Logging a day takes about thirty seconds, and each day can be shared as a card sized for Instagram Stories.

The app works without an account. There are no ads or analytics, and journals sync only through the user's own iCloud.
