Platforms

We build focused iPhone apps for travelers. Each one does a single job well and keeps your data private.

Go: TravelApp

Your smart travel companion

Go: TravelApp keeps the details of a trip in one place: passports and visas with reminders before they expire, trip plans, visa requirement checks based on the passports you hold, and pre-trip checklists.

Its travel cockpit shows the essentials for your destination, including plug types, currency, language, time zone and safety levels. Available on the App Store.

Visit gotravelapp.com

TripLog Mini

A travel journal for iPhone

TripLog Mini turns each day of a trip into three things: one photo, one meal or drink, and one sentence. Logging a day takes about thirty seconds, and each day can be shared as a card sized for Instagram Stories.

You don't need an account; Sign in with Apple is optional. There are no ads or analytics, and journals sync only through your own iCloud. Coming soon to the App Store.
