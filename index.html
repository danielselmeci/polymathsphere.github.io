What we do

Polymath Sphere designs, builds and runs software. Most of our work goes into our own products, focused travel apps for iPhone. The rest goes into selected client projects in apps, websites and design.

The company was founded by entrepreneur and photographer Daniel Selmeci. It is privately held and independently operated, so we can keep our products small, careful with your data, and supported for the long term.
