About

Polymath Sphere LLC is a software and design company based in Arkansas. It was founded by entrepreneur and photographer Daniel Selmeci as the home for his apps and creative work.

How we operate

The company is privately held and independently operated. No outside investor sets our pace, so we can keep products focused, treat privacy as a default rather than an add-on, and support what we release for the long term.

What we make

Our own products are travel apps for iPhone: Go: TravelApp and TripLog Mini.

Alongside them, the studio takes on a limited number of client projects in apps, websites and design.
