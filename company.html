Company

Formation

Polymath Sphere LLC was formed as an Arkansas-based technical innovation venture and digital media agency — a private structure for software, applications, and creative media.

Structure

The company operates without external investment pressure and prioritizes durability, clarity, and regulatory alignment.

Founded by entrepreneur and photographer Daniel Selmeci, it serves as the foundational umbrella for creative projects and high-utility mobile utilities.

Scope

Polymath Sphere specializes in software development, application design, and creative media production.

The firm is committed to bridging structural software solutions with global lifestyle and travel needs, housing platforms such as the GO: Travel App.
